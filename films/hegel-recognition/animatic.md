# Lord and Bondsman: animatic (revision 3.1)

Rough storyboard for a timing animatic before final art: one held drawing per shot (two to four key poses where noted), graphite or plain ink on paper, cut to a scratch read of the narration in script.md. Square frame. RC is the red-coat traveller (later the lord), SH the straw-hat traveller (later the bondsman); draw them the same size, SH's hat on in every shot. Total 120 seconds, 14 shots. Test in the animatic: can someone who has not read the script tell, with sound off, who wins, why the other gives in, who serves, and what he finds in the jar?

1. 0:00 to 0:08 (8 s). Wide: hill country, two roads curving down from left and right to a tiny plank bridge over a gorge; title "Lord and Bondsman" in the sky. Slow push in.
2. 0:08 to 0:19 (11 s). Split square: RC, pack on his back, eats a pear under a tree (left); SH drinks at a stream (right). Key pose 2: RC's hand reaching up to the next tree, SH's hand on his stomach. Key pose 3: close-up of the half-eaten pear in RC's hand, just sitting there. Key pose 4: the split line slides away and both walk toward the bridge from opposite ends.
3. 0:19 to 0:29 (10 s). Side view, bridge over the drop: RC and SH stopped face to face in the middle, identical stances; slow move in to a profile two-shot; quotation card "It exists only in being acknowledged." Hegel, ¶178.
4. 0:29 to 0:36 (7 s). Close on four feet toe to toe on the planks, SH's back heel lifting and planting, RC's staff tip tapping once; then the two-shot, each looking the other up and down like a cart in the road.
5. 0:36 to 0:44 (8 s). Wide, bridge swaying, four clear key poses held: they seize each other by the shoulders; RC bent back over the rope rail; SH bent back; the staff falling toward the river.
6. 0:44 to 0:51 (7 s). Freeze, bridge tilted: both over the rail, gripping each other, evenly matched; RC's eyes on SH, SH's eyes on the water. 1 s insert of the empty bridge with RC alone; back to the freeze, 1.5 s of silence.
7. 0:51 to 1:00 (9 s). Close on SH's hand letting go of RC and grabbing the rope rail; key pose 2, the rope darkening; key pose 3, wider, the bridge steady, RC standing, SH low and holding the rail.
8. 1:00 to 1:11 (11 s). RC holding out his pack, SH shouldering it, the strap across his chest, both walking off the bridge with SH two steps behind; cut to the porch days later: RC seated and content with bread, SH bowing as he sets down a cup; eyeline insert: the top of a straw hat.
9. 1:11 to 1:19 (8 s). SH, strap across his chest, walking up from the river with a full jug and pouring it into RC's cup on the porch. Plain daylight.
10. 1:19 to 1:24 (5 s). RC pointing at the clay bank, then at his cup; SH digging clay by the river and setting it on a kick wheel; quotation card "Work ... is desire held in check." Hegel, ¶195.
11. 1:24 to 1:28 (4 s). Close-up: two hands raising clay on the wheel, four fingertips pressing four parallel horizontal throwing rings into the turning wall; the rings must read clearly.
12. 1:28 to 1:35 (7 s). Porch: the finished jar; RC drinks from a cup poured from it, sets the cup down empty and goes inside; SH reaches for the jar.
13. 1:35 to 1:42 (7 s). Extreme close-up: SH's four fingers sliding into the four rings and fitting exactly; 2 s silent hold in plain light with faint pencil traces of his hands; key pose 2, he turns the jar and looks at it; quotation card "He acquires a mind of his own." Hegel, ¶196.
14. 1:42 to 2:00 (18 s). From outside: the dark doorway with a hand raised in it; SH sets the jar on the step, shoulders the strap and goes in; hold on the jar alone; pull back and up to the whole valley at dusk (house, river, bridge, both roads); end card "Hegel, Phenomenology of Spirit (1807), paragraphs 178 to 196" from 1:58.
