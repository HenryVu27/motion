Lord and Bondsman

Hegel on recognition: why a self needs another self, why forced recognition is hollow, and what the bondsman finds in his work. Work in progress: beats 1 and 2 of 9.

About this film

Hegel, Phenomenology of Spirit (1807), chapter IV A, paragraphs 178 to 196. Written by the studio's scriptwriter agent from the German text and Miller's translation, reviewed by an adversarial critic, and checked claim by claim in sources.md. The film stops where Hegel's section stops: the bondsman finds a mind of his own and is still a bondsman. Readings by Kojève and Marx are not Hegel's and are not shown as his.
