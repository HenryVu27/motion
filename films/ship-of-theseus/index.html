The Ship of Theseus

Thirty-six seconds, every frame drawn by JavaScript: paper, ink, halftone, risograph and watercolor, all from code.

How it is made

  • Every frame is a function of time. Each scene is draw(ctx, { t }); nothing is stored between frames, so the player can scrub and the exporter renders exactly what you see.
  • Print, faked honestly. Flat inks are drawn on their own layer, a speckle mask eats grain out of them, and they are multiplied onto the paper, sometimes a few pixels off register, the way a risograph prints.
  • Tone without gradients. Shadows are pencil hatching, rot is stippling, the sea and the sun's glow are halftone dots. Tone comes from how many marks there are, not from smooth color.
  • Seeded randomness. Wobble, grain and plank order come from fixed seeds, so the hand-drawn look holds still instead of boiling.
  • Export in the browser. Export MP4 renders every frame, encodes H.264 with WebCodecs and packages it with mediabunny. No server, no ffmpeg.
