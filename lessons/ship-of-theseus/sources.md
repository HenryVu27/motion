## Illustration choices (labelled on the page)
- The drawing has 24 planks and 15 visible oars, one side of a thirty-oared galley. The real ship's construction is unknown.
- The page compresses years of repair into one scroll, and shows every plank replaced (Hobbes's version, not Plutarch's).
- The verdict texts after the reader's choice are the page's own interpretation, except the quoted Hobbes lines.

## Do not copy from
Wikipedia's "Ship of Theseus" blockquote of Hobbes has small transcription errors ("restoration" for "reparation", "had" grammar). Quote the 1656 scan or Molesworth.
