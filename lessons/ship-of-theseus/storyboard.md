# Ship of Theseus: storyboard

- **Idea in one sentence:** Whether something is "the same thing" depends on what its name picks out: its matter, its form, or its continuous history.
- **Blind spot:** People assume identity is a fact about the object, so the question must have one right answer.
- **The click:** After every plank of the ship has been replaced, the discarded planks fly back together, in the same order, into a second ship beside the first. Two ships, one name.
- **What the viewer does:** Scrolls the replacement plank by plank (they control the gradual change), then must pick which ship is the Ship of Theseus before Hobbes answers.
