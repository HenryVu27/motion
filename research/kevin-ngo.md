# Kevin Ngo (Kevo, @kevopuff)

- Co-creator of A-Frame (github.com/aframevr/aframe, about 17.7k stars), the HTML-first WebVR/WebXR framework from Mozilla. Previously Y Combinator and Mozilla (X bio). Los Angeles.
- Animation work: in 2018 he rewrote A-Frame's animation component to animate property values directly instead of going through setAttribute and a component update each frame. His post reports a stress test going from 15 to 45 FPS with large memory savings and 1000 fewer lines (x.com/kevopuff/status/1038935828146188288). Lesson: in a hot animation loop, skip the framework's reactive layer and write the value.
- gogh (github.com/ngokevin/gogh, demo ngokevin.github.io/gogh): procedural landscape painting on Canvas. Drill 10 studies its code.
- Moonrider (github.com/supermedium/moonrider): WebXR rhythm game.
- Now: Coupling Cafe (couplingcafe.com), a language-learning app for couples, built with Heidi. Cozy cafe and garden theme. Substack: coupling.substack.com.
