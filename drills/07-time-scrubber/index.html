Motion Studio / Drill 07

Scrubbing through time

Make the year the control. Drag a line through twenty-five centuries and watch who was alive at the same moment.

What this teaches

  • There is no year 0. Store historical years (negative for BCE), convert to astronomical numbering (1 BCE = 0) before any scale or subtraction, and convert back only for labels. Otherwise every span that crosses the era boundary is a year too long.
  • Direct manipulation follows the finger, programmatic jumps animate. While dragging, the line sits exactly under the pointer with no transition. A button press tweens the year itself with d3, so the highlights sweep through every year on the way.
  • Draw uncertainty, do not footnote it. A circa end fades out; a traditional date is dashed. The reader sees which bars are documented lives and which are estimates before reading a word.
  • Transition the state, not the frame. Each change sets a new target colour and d3 interrupts the running transition on that element, so a fast scrub never queues up a backlog of fades.
  • Keyboard and screen readers get the same control. The range input under the chart is the real slider; dragging the chart just writes to it, and the list of who is alive is a live region.

Data

Birth and death years were checked on 5 November 2026 against English Wikipedia, with the Stanford and Internet Encyclopedias of Philosophy for the earliest Chinese figures; the source of each row is in data.js. A year marked c. in the source is drawn with a faded end. Laozi's dates are a tradition, not a life: the dashed bar spans the sixth century BCE where the tradition places him. Heraclitus is left out because the sources give only a floruit of about 500 BCE.
